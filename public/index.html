How far off could the “% counted” estimate be? The break-even range and the grey band on the meter use this. Most people can leave it at ± 3.

Read this before trusting the math

  • “Percent counted” is an estimate of total turnout, not a hard number. The range assumes it could be off by 3 points either way (change that under Advanced). Early in the night it can miss by more, so try dragging it.
  • Uncounted votes are rarely a random sample. Late mail, provisional ballots and specific counties can break very differently from the early count.
  • This is a what-if calculator, not a forecast. It doesn’t predict who will win.
