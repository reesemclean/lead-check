Read this before trusting the math

  • “Percent counted” is an estimate of total turnout, not a hard number. Use the uncertainty setting.
  • Uncounted votes are rarely a random sample. Late mail, provisional ballots and specific counties can break very differently from the early count.
  • This is a what-if calculator, not a forecast. It doesn’t predict who will win.
